About us

Four machines for groundwork, chosen carefully and described honestly

GroundVero is a UK online shop for contractors who work on tight, finished sites. We sell four machines — a mini excavator, a 500 kg tracked dumper, a petrol wood chipper and a stump grinder — because four is a range we can describe accurately, down to the width across the tracks.

We do not hold stock ourselves. Each machine is crated at our supplier's warehouse overseas and delivered free to the UK mainland in 7–12 working days. The price you see is the whole price: nothing is added at checkout and nothing is collected at your door.

Behind that sits a 30-day return, two years on parts, one year on the engine, and a team that answers the phone Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
